# NDR-001 長いDNSラベルを持つ多数の異なる問い合わせ

50〜63文字のDNSラベルを含む問い合わせが5分で20種類以上。

|項目|内容|
|---|---|
|対象ログ|NDR|
|必要フィールド|`_time`, `dns_query`, `log_type`, `protocol`, `src_ip`|
|ATT&CK 戦術|[TA0011 Command and Control](https://attack.mitre.org/tactics/TA0011)|
|技法 → サブ技法|[T1071 Application Layer Protocol](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1071) → [T1071.004 DNS](https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1071/004)|

## SPL

```spl
index=detection_lab log_type="ndr" earliest=-10m@m latest=-5m@m
| where isnotnull(_time)
    AND isnotnull(dns_query)
    AND isnotnull(log_type)
    AND isnotnull(protocol)
    AND isnotnull(src_ip)
    AND protocol="dns"
    AND match(dns_query, "(^|\\.)[^.]{50,63}(\\.|$)")
| stats dc(dns_query) as observed min(_time) as first_seen max(_time) as last_seen by src_ip
| where observed >= 20
| eval rule_id="NDR-001", attack_id="T1071.004"
```

**範囲：** DNS本文の可視性が必要。短いラベルやDoH/DoTは対象外。
